Yo, here's the tea on ANITS (Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology and Sciences). It's an autonomous engineering college out in Sangivalasa, near Vizag, that popped up back in 2001. Going pro-teacher mode for a sec: the place flexes a shiny NAAC "A+" grade and is affiliated with Andhra University, heavily focusing on B.Tech programs like CSE, AI/ML, and Data Science. It sits on a 12-acre campus where students are basically grinding through assignments and labs trying to survive those placements. Lowkey, it's a solid spot if you're trying to secure the bag in the tech world without totally losing your mind.

Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology & Sciences (ANITS), Visakhapatnam is owned by Megha Engineering and Infrastructure Limited (MEIL) which is one of the top infrastructure and manufacturing company in India with headquarters at Hyderabad. The institute was established in the academic year 2001-02 by Anil Neerukonda Educational Society (ANES) which was founded by Dr. N.B.R. Prasad, an NRI Philanthropist from the USA, with industrialists and eminent educationists in memory of his son Late Anil Neerukonda.
